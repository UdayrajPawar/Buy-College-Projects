FAQ

Questions students usually ask

How long does a typical project take?

Most projects are delivered in 2–4 weeks depending on complexity. Timelines are agreed and fixed at kickoff, so you always know your delivery date up front.

Do you provide source code and documentation?

Yes — every project ships with full commented source code, a synopsis, the final report, a presentation deck, and an IEEE-format paper where required.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Yes. Revisions and bug fixes are included free of charge until your project is formally accepted by your evaluators.

Do you help with viva and demo preparation?

Every project includes a dedicated prep session covering the questions your panel is most likely to ask, plus a run-through of your live demo.

Can you match my college's specific formatting rules?

Yes — send us your college's format guide and we'll match the report, synopsis, and PPT to it exactly.

Is remote support available for setup and testing?

Yes, most support happens over call and WhatsApp, including live screen-share sessions when you're setting up hardware or debugging code.
